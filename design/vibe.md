Use scene: a recruiter opens a link from LinkedIn or a resume, on a laptop, once, for one to three minutes.
Adjectives: futuristic, quiet, precise. Visual first, very few words.
Not: a website (no nav bars, cards, sections of paragraphs), AI-generated looking, neon.
References: igloo.inc (one continuous 3D world, the camera flies through it as you scroll; words are small labels in the world).
Theme: dark space, light objects.
Photos: floating in 3D space; the nearest one sharpens and gets a few words.
Density: airy. One idea on screen at a time.
Expression: distinctive (the user asked for cool scrolling effects).
Hard nos: sound, custom cursor. Plus the rejected v1 look: mono eyebrow labels, 01/02 numbering, paragraphs, cream + serif.
